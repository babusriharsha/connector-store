How pricing works

1

Free for the first month

Every connector starts with a free month from the day you activate it. No card is needed to start the trial — connect, use it on your real business, and judge it on results.

2

Per-connector monthly price

After the trial, each connector has its own monthly price, because the value differs — a store connector that saves you hours a week is priced differently from a small utility. You subscribe only to the ones you keep. ShopSignal for WooCommerce: $19/month per store. SellSignal for Etsy: coming soon (planned $9/month per shop). ListSignal for Mailchimp: $15/month per account. There is no bundle and no all-access pass: as the catalog grows toward 100+ connectors, each one is priced and subscribed to on its own.

Billing

  • Payments are processed by Stripe — the store never handles or stores your card details. Your subscription is with CALL SNAP SYSTEMS LLC, and that is the name that appears on your card statement.
  • Subscriptions are per connector and renew monthly until cancelled.
  • Your trial is tracked by the connector itself, on your own machine. Ask your Muse at any time and the connector will tell you exactly how many free days remain.

Cancelling

  • Cancel anytime. There is no contract and no cancellation fee.
  • A cancelled connector keeps working until the end of the paid period, then pauses.
  • If a trial ends and you don’t subscribe, the connector simply pauses. Nothing is charged — there is no card on file unless you added one.
  • Refund policy: if you email us at vbshmedia@gmail.com within 14 days of any charge and ask for a refund, we will refund that charge in full to your original payment method. After 14 days, charges are not refundable — but cancelling stops all future charges, and your connector keeps working until the end of the paid period.
