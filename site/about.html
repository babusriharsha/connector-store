About Plugvana

Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta or with any platform our connectors work with.

Why this store exists

Muse is a personal AI agent that gets more useful the more of your world it can reach. Some services already ship their own way to connect. Many of the tools small businesses actually run on — a WooCommerce store, an Etsy shop, a Mailchimp account — had no answer when their owner asked their Muse, “can you connect to this?”

Plugvana builds those missing connectors: real, maintained software that links a service to a user’s Muse, installed by the user’s own Muse from our public repository, with secure sign-in and read-only-by-default safety rules.

How we work

Honest by design. Every connector runs inside the customer’s own Muse. We don’t store their business data — there is nothing on our side to leak, sell, or lose. Our hosted footprint is deliberately small: this website, the public connector repository, and a license check that tracks installs and trials, the way an app store counts downloads.

Priced per connector. Each connector is free for its first month, then carries its own monthly price based on the value it delivers. There is no bundle and no all-access pass — with a catalog growing toward 100+ connectors, you simply pay only for the ones you use.

Built in the open. The store’s builder shares the build publicly — what each connector does, what it costs to run, and what’s coming next.
