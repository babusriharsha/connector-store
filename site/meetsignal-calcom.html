MeetSignal for Cal.com

Who's booked you today? MeetSignal answers from your Cal.com: upcoming bookings with attendee details, your event types and working schedules — and it can cancel a booking only when you explicitly approve that exact cancellation.

Today's bookings

Who, when, and which event type — in one answer.

How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Cal.com with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Cal.com with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A Cal.com account you administer.
  • Your Cal.com API key, saved through Muse's secure flow.

MeetSignal for Cal.com — questions

Does it work with Cal.com's free plan?

API keys are available on Cal.com accounts; bookings and event types read the same.

Can it reschedule?

v1 reads bookings and cancels on explicit approval; rescheduling stays in Cal.com.

Team event types?

Your account's event types are listed; team scoping follows your API key's access.
