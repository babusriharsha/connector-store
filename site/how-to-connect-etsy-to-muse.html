How to connect Etsy to Muse

Muse doesn’t connect to Etsy on its own — you add a connector. The SellSignal for Etsy from Plugvana links your shop to your Muse personal AI agent using Etsy’s own secure sign-in, and your own Muse does the installing. Here is the whole process:

Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta or with any platform our connectors work with.

1

Open your Muse and say it

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Etsy shop with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds the SellSignal for Etsy in this store and presents it to you as a card — tap Connect on the card to start.

2

Approve on Etsy’s sign-in page

Your Muse sends you to Etsy’s own secure OAuth page. You sign in and approve the connection there — your Etsy password never passes through chat.

3

Confirm with a live snapshot

Your Muse runs a health check and shows your last 7 days of sales and orders, your lowest-quantity listings, and your latest review — all from your real shop. If they match, you’re connected.

After that, try: “How did my shop do this week?”, “Which listings are running low?”, or “Give me my Morning Shop Brief.” The connector is free for the first month, no card needed.
