ImageSignal

Need a folder of product photos at the right size? ImageSignal reports dimensions across a whole folder, resizes and converts PNG, BMP and PPM images, and reads JPEG dimensions — all locally in your Muse, with the format limits stated up front.

Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta or with any platform our connectors work with.

ImageSignal is an independent tool by CALL SNAP SYSTEMS LLC. It works on your own image files; no platform account is involved.

What it does

Folder reports

Format, dimensions and file size for every image in a folder — find the odd ones out fast.

Resize

Scale PNG, BMP and PPM images to exact dimensions you specify.

Convert

Convert between PNG, BMP and PPM without uploading to a random website.

JPEG info

Dimensions and size for JPEGs (resize for JPEG arrives in a later version — stated honestly).

Batch work

Point it at a folder, not one file at a time.

Originals preserved

Resized and converted images are new files; originals stay untouched.

How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my image tools with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my image tools with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • Image files in your Muse's workspace.
  • No account or API key.

Safety notes

  • No uploads. Images are processed locally — your photos never go to a third-party converter site.
  • Originals kept. Outputs are new files.
  • Honest scope. If a format or operation isn't supported in v1, the connector says so instead of producing a broken file.

ImageSignal — questions

Can it resize JPEG photos?

Not yet in v1 — it reads JPEG dimensions and info. PNG/BMP/PPM resizing is fully supported.

Can it compress for the web?

Converting formats and resizing down will cut file size a lot; dedicated JPEG/WebP compression is on the roadmap.

Does it work on a whole folder?

Yes — batch info and batch resize are core features.
