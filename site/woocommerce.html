What it does

Store snapshot

Today vs yesterday vs last week: revenue, order count, and average order value — one question, one clear answer.

Orders & refunds

Recent orders, lookup by order number, customer name, or email; unfulfilled orders; refund totals for any period, with a flag when the refund rate spikes.

Products & best sellers

Search your catalog, check any product’s details and variants, see best sellers by period, and spot dead stock that hasn’t sold in weeks.

Inventory & low-stock alerts

Stock levels by product and variant, an out-of-stock list, and low-stock alerts against a threshold you set — before you sell something you don’t have.

Customers & win-back list

Top customers by spend, repeat-buyer picture, and the lapsed list — customers who haven’t ordered in 60 or 90 days — ready for a win-back offer.

Morning Store Brief

A daily summary from your Muse: yesterday’s numbers, unfulfilled orders, low stock, and anything unusual — silent on the routine, loud on the exceptions.

Coupons & edits — with your approval

Create a coupon, or adjust a price or stock level. Your Muse shows you the exact change first; nothing runs until you approve it.

Period reports

“Last month vs the month before” across sales, products, and customers — compared for you, in plain English, not raw tables.

How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my WooCommerce store with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my WooCommerce store with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then sign in on WooCommerce’s own secure page (or through Muse’s secure connection flow). Your Muse confirms the connection with a live snapshot of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent (the one you already chat with).
  • A WooCommerce store on WordPress that you administer.
  • Five minutes to create REST API keys in WP Admin when your Muse asks.
  • No card needed for the free month.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $19/month per store and shown here before subscriptions open. When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • Credentials never pass through chat. Your REST API keys go through Muse’s secure connection flow only.
  • Read-only by default. The connector reads your store. Write actions (coupons, price or stock edits) are offered only when you ask, shown to you first, and run only after your approval. One honest detail about the key itself: the standard WooCommerce REST API key this connector asks you to create is a Read/Write key. The connector only ever reads unless you approve a specific write action first — but the key itself can write. If you never want writes at all, create a Read key instead: every read feature works, and the write actions simply won’t be available.
  • Your data stays yours. The connector runs inside your own Muse. This store never receives your orders, customers, or revenue figures — only install and trial/subscription status.
  • Revoke anytime. Delete the API keys in WP Admin and ask your Muse to remove the connector; access ends immediately.

ShopSignal for WooCommerce — questions

Do I need to install a WordPress plugin?

No. The connector uses WooCommerce’s built-in REST API. You create API keys in WP Admin — nothing is installed on your site.

Can it work with more than one store?

Yes — connect each store with its own keys, and your Muse will ask which store you mean when a question is ambiguous.

Will it slow my site down?

No. It makes the same kind of requests your own admin dashboard makes, only when you ask your Muse something.

What happens at the end of the free month?

The connector pauses until you subscribe. Your keys and setup stay in place, so subscribing resumes everything instantly.
