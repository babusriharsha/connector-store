How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my FreshBooks with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my FreshBooks with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A FreshBooks account you administer.
  • A few minutes to approve the FreshBooks sign-in (OAuth) through Muse's secure flow.
