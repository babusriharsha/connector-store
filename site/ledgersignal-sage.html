Invoices in full

Look up any sales or purchase invoice by id and see its lines and status.

Bank accounts

Your Sage bank accounts and their balances in one answer.

How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Sage accounting with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Sage accounting with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A Sage Business Cloud Accounting subscription you administer.
  • A few minutes to approve the Sage sign-in (OAuth) through Muse's secure flow.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $19/month per business (or $190/year — two months free). When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • Credentials never pass through chat. Sage sign-in happens on Sage's own pages via Muse's secure connection flow.
  • Read-first. The only write is a draft invoice, shown to you and created only after your approval.
  • Your books stay yours. Data flows between Sage and your own Muse; this store receives only install and subscription status.

LedgerSignal for Sage — questions

Which Sage product is this for?

Sage Business Cloud Accounting (the cloud product with the v3.1 API). Desktop Sage 50 is a different product and is not supported.

Can it send invoices to customers?

No — it creates drafts inside Sage. You review and send from Sage itself.

What happens after the free month?

The connector pauses until you subscribe. Nothing is ever charged silently.
