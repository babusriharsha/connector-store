SheetSignal

Every business has one messy spreadsheet. SheetSignal cleans it inside your Muse: trim the junk, drop duplicate rows, merge the monthly exports, and turn any CSV into a summary you can actually read — totals, averages and missing-value counts included.

Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta or with any platform our connectors work with.

SheetSignal is an independent tool by CALL SNAP SYSTEMS LLC. It works on your own spreadsheet files; no platform account is involved.

What it does

Clean

Trim whitespace, drop empty rows and normalise headers across a whole file at once.

Dedupe

Remove duplicate rows by any column — keep the first, keep the latest, you choose.

Merge

Stack several CSVs with the same columns into one clean file.

Convert

CSV to JSON or Markdown tables and back — handy for reports and docs.

Summaries

Row and column counts, numeric totals and averages, and where the blanks are hiding.

XLSX reading

Read Excel .xlsx files and export them to CSV — no Excel install needed.

How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my spreadsheet tools with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my spreadsheet tools with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • CSV or XLSX files in your Muse's workspace.
  • No account or API key.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $5/month per user (or $50/year — two months free). When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • Local only. Your spreadsheets never leave your Muse's workspace.
  • New files, not overwrites. Cleaning and converting write new files so the original export is always intact.
  • Your numbers stay private. This store receives no rows, totals or file names — only install and subscription status.

SheetSignal — questions

Can it edit .xlsx files directly?

v1 reads XLSX and writes CSV/JSON/Markdown. Writing native .xlsx is on the roadmap.

What counts as a duplicate?

You pick the column (or columns) that define a duplicate — email address, order id, whatever fits your data.

How big a file can it handle?

Everyday business exports — thousands to hundreds of thousands of rows — are fine.
