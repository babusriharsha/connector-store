How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Zoho CRM with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Zoho CRM with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A Zoho CRM organisation you administer.
  • A few minutes to approve the Zoho sign-in (OAuth) through Muse's secure flow; your Zoho datacenter (zohoapis.com / .eu / .in) is registered at install.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $15/month per organisation (or $150/year — two months free). When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • OAuth on Zoho's own pages. Passwords never pass through chat.
  • Read-first. Lead creation is approval-gated.
  • Your CRM data stays between Zoho and your Muse.

ClientSignal for Zoho CRM — questions

I'm on Zoho's EU datacenter — supported?

Yes. The installing Muse registers your own zohoapis host (com, eu or in) at connect time.

Does it cover other Zoho apps?

v1 is Zoho CRM. Other Zoho apps are separate connectors on the roadmap.

Can it bulk-edit records?

No — reads, search, and one approval-gated create. No bulk writes, ever.
