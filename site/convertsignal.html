ConvertSignal

Stop pasting documents into random converter websites. ConvertSignal moves your writing between plain text, Markdown, HTML and Word .docx — extracting docx text, generating clean HTML from Markdown, and stripping HTML back to readable text — inside your own Muse.

Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta or with any platform our connectors work with.

ConvertSignal is an independent tool by CALL SNAP SYSTEMS LLC. It works on your own documents; no platform account is involved.

What it does

Markdown to HTML

Headings, emphasis, links, lists and code blocks become clean, valid HTML.

Word text extraction

Pull the full text out of .docx files for editing, summarising or republishing.

HTML to text

Strip a web page file down to its readable words.

Info at a glance

Word counts and format details for any document.

Batch conversions

Convert a whole folder of notes or drafts in one ask.

Local and private

Documents are converted in your Muse's workspace, never uploaded.

How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my document converter with Plugvana”

Safety notes

  • Local processing. Your documents never leave your Muse.
  • New outputs. Conversions write new files; originals are preserved.
  • Honest subset. The Markdown converter covers the common formatting honestly; exotic extensions are passed through as text, not silently dropped.

ConvertSignal — questions

Can it create .docx files?

v1 extracts text from .docx and converts between txt/Markdown/HTML. Native .docx writing is on the roadmap.

Will my formatting survive?

Common Markdown formatting — headings, lists, links, bold/italic, code — converts cleanly. Complex Word styling simplifies to clean text.

Is it safe for confidential drafts?

Yes — nothing is uploaded anywhere; conversion happens in your own Muse.
