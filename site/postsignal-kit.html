How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Kit account with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Kit account with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A Kit (ConvertKit) account you administer.
  • Your Kit API key, saved through Muse's secure flow.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $15/month per account (or $150/year — two months free). When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • API key via secure flow only.
  • Read-first. Tagging is the only write and it is approval-gated.
  • Your audience data stays between Kit and your Muse.

PostSignal for Kit — questions

I still call it ConvertKit — same thing?

Yes. Kit is the new name for ConvertKit; this connector speaks to the same account and API.

Can it write my broadcasts?

Muse can draft in chat; PostSignal reads performance and manages tags — sending stays in Kit.

Free Kit plan?

API access is included; growth numbers work the same.
