How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my AWeber with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my AWeber with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • An AWeber account you administer.
  • A few minutes to approve the AWeber sign-in (OAuth) through Muse's secure flow.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $15/month per account (or $150/year — two months free). When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • OAuth on AWeber's own pages.
  • Opt-in discipline. Adds require confirmed opt-in, always.
  • Your list data stays between AWeber and your Muse.

NewsSignal for AWeber — questions

Multiple lists?

Yes — commands take a list selector, and Muse asks which list when it's ambiguous.

Can it create broadcasts?

v1 reads performance and manages subscribers on approval; creating broadcasts stays in AWeber.

Legacy follow-ups vs campaigns?

Both are visible — AWeber's API exposes broadcasts and campaign series per list.
