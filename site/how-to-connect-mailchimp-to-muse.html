How to connect Mailchimp to Muse

Muse doesn’t connect to Mailchimp on its own — you add a connector. The ListSignal for Mailchimp from Plugvana links your account to your Muse personal AI agent, and your own Muse does the installing. Here is the whole process:

Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta or with any platform our connectors work with.

1

Open your Muse and say it

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Mailchimp account with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds the ListSignal for Mailchimp in this store and presents it to you as a card — tap Connect on the card to start.

2

Create a Mailchimp API key

Your Muse gives you the exact clicks to create an API key in your Mailchimp account settings. Enter it through Muse’s secure connection flow — never paste a key into a chat message.

3

Confirm with a live snapshot

Your Muse runs a health check and shows your audiences with subscriber counts and growth, plus your most recent campaign’s opens, clicks, and bounces in plain numbers. If they match Mailchimp, you’re connected.

After that, try: “Is my list growing?”, “How did my last campaign actually do?”, or “Which campaign performed best this quarter?” The connector can also prepare draft campaigns — it never sends. Free for the first month, no card needed.
