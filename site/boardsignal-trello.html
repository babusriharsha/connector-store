How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Trello with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Trello with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A Trello account you administer.
  • Your Trello API key and token, saved through Muse's secure flow (never in chat).

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $9/month per account (or $90/year — two months free). When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • Key and token via secure flow only.
  • Read-first. Card creation is the only write and it is approval-gated.
  • Your board data stays between Trello and your Muse.
