How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my MailerLite with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my MailerLite with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A MailerLite account you administer.
  • Your MailerLite API token, saved through Muse's secure flow.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $15/month per account (or $150/year — two months free). When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • Token via secure flow only.
  • Opt-in discipline. The add-subscriber command requires you to confirm the person opted in; bought or scraped lists are refused by design.
  • Your list data stays between MailerLite and your Muse.

SendSignal for MailerLite — questions

Can it send campaigns?

v1 reads performance and manages subscribers on approval; sending stays in MailerLite.

Does it work with MailerLite's free plan?

Yes, API tokens are available on free accounts.

Can it import a list I bought?

No. Only confirmed opt-ins — that's a hard rule in this connector.
