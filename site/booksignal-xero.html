How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Xero with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Xero with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A Xero organisation you administer.
  • A few minutes to approve the Xero sign-in (OAuth) through Muse's secure flow.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $19/month per organisation (or $190/year — two months free). When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • OAuth via Xero's own pages. Your Xero password never passes through chat or this store.
  • Read-first. Draft invoice creation is the only write, and it is approval-gated.
  • Your data stays between Xero and your Muse. This store receives only install and subscription status.

BookSignal for Xero — questions

Does it work with multiple Xero organisations?

One subscription covers one organisation — the one you connect. You can add another organisation with its own subscription.

Can it file my taxes?

No. It reads and drafts; filing stays with you and your accountant.

Can it pay bills?

No command moves money. Ever.
