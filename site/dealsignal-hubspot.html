How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my HubSpot with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my HubSpot with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A HubSpot account (free CRM works) you administer.
  • A few minutes to create a private-app token in HubSpot and save it through Muse's secure flow.

Safety notes

  • Token via secure flow. Your HubSpot private-app token never passes through chat.
  • Read-first. The only write — adding a contact — is approval-gated.
  • Your CRM data stays between HubSpot and your Muse.

DealSignal for HubSpot — questions

Does the free HubSpot CRM work?

Yes — private app tokens are available on free accounts.

Can it email my contacts?

No. DealSignal reads your CRM and drafts; sending stays in your tools.

Can it update deal stages?

v1 is read-first with contact creation on approval; stage moves are on the roadmap.
