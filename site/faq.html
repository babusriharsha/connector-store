No. The Connector Store is an independent store run by CALL SNAP SYSTEMS LLC. Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta, WooCommerce, Automattic, Etsy, or Mailchimp. Our connectors use the same public, documented ways of connecting that Muse supports for everyone.

What exactly is a connector?

A connector is software that links a service you use — a store, an email marketing account — to your Muse personal AI agent. Once connected, your Muse can read that service’s data for you and, with your approval, take small actions in it. Think of it as giving your Muse a secure window into one service at a time.

Where does my data go? Does the store see my store data?

No. A connector runs inside your own Muse and talks directly to the service you connected. Your orders, customers, and campaigns are never sent to or stored by this store. The only things the store tracks are the connector itself: that it was installed, its version, and whether its trial or subscription is active — the same way an app store counts installs.

Do I ever type my password or keys into a chat?

Never. You sign in on the service’s own secure page, or enter a key you created through Muse’s secure connection flow. Credentials never pass through chat — if anyone ever asks you to paste a password into a chat message, stop.

Can a connector change things in my store or account?

Only with your approval, and only the specific change shown to you. Connectors are read-only by default. Actions like creating a coupon or editing a price are presented in your own Muse first, and nothing runs until you say yes.

How does the free month work?

Every connector is free for its first month, counted from the day you activate it. No card is needed to start. Use it on your real business for the full month.

What does the free month cost me — is there a catch?

Nothing. The trial is genuinely free: there is no card on file, so there is nothing to charge. The connector simply pauses when the month ends unless you choose to subscribe.

What happens when the trial ends?

The connector pauses. Your data and your connection setup stay in place, and the connector resumes the moment you subscribe. Nothing is deleted, and nothing is charged automatically.

How much do connectors cost after the trial?

Each connector has its own monthly price — see Pricing. Prices are shown on each connector’s page before you subscribe: WooCommerce $19/month, Etsy $9/month, Mailchimp $15/month. There is no bundle — each connector is subscribed to on its own.

How do I cancel? What about refunds?

Cancel anytime — there is no contract. A cancelled connector keeps working until the end of the paid period, then pauses. Billing is handled by Stripe. Refund policy: if you email us at vbshmedia@gmail.com within 14 days of any charge and ask for a refund, we will refund that charge in full to your original payment method. After 14 days, charges are not refundable — but cancelling stops all future charges, and your connector keeps working until the end of the paid period.

How do I disconnect a connector completely?

Ask your Muse to remove the connector, and revoke its access on the service’s side as well (for example, delete the API keys in WooCommerce or Mailchimp, or disconnect the app in Etsy). Both steps together cut access completely.

Which connectors are available now?

Three: WooCommerce, Etsy, and Mailchimp. More are in development, chosen by what owners ask for most.
