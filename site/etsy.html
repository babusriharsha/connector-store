Etsy Connector

Connect your Etsy shop to your Muse personal AI agent. Ask “How did my shop do this week?”, “Which listings are running low?”, “What did buyers say?” — and your Muse answers from your real shop data. Looking for steps? See how to connect Etsy to Muse.

What it does

Shop snapshot

Built from your receipts: sales and order count for today, this week, or any period — compared with the period before, in plain words.

Orders

Recent orders and their status, lookup by buyer or item, and a clear list of what still needs to ship.

Listings & inventory

Your active listings with their quantities, so you can see at a glance what’s stocked, what’s thin, and what has sold out.

Low-quantity alerts

Tell your Muse your comfort level per listing; it flags anything at or below it — before a best seller quietly hits zero.

Reviews

Recent reviews in one place, with an honest read on the pattern — what buyers love, and what keeps coming up.

Payment ledger

Your Etsy payment ledger summarized: what came in, what fees took, and what’s due to you — without opening five dashboard screens.

Morning Shop Brief

A daily summary from your Muse: yesterday’s sales, orders to ship, low quantities, and new reviews — the whole shop in one short message.

Period reports

“This month vs last month” for sales, orders, and top listings — compared for you, in plain English.

How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Etsy shop with The Connector Store”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Etsy shop with The Connector Store”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then sign in on Etsy’s own secure page (or through Muse’s secure connection flow). Your Muse confirms the connection with a live snapshot of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent (the one you already chat with).
  • An Etsy shop where you are the owner.
  • A minute to approve the connection on Etsy’s sign-in page.
  • No card needed for the free month.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $9/month per shop and shown here before subscriptions open. When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • Credentials never pass through chat. You sign in on Etsy’s own page; Etsy issues a limited token, and your password is never shared with the connector or this store.
  • Read-only by default. The connector reads your shop — receipts, listings, reviews, ledger. It does not change listings, prices, or orders.
  • Your data stays yours. The connector runs inside your own Muse. This store never receives your sales, buyers, or reviews — only install and trial/subscription status.
  • Revoke anytime. Disconnect the app from your Etsy account settings and ask your Muse to remove the connector; access ends immediately.

Etsy Connector — questions

Does Etsy allow this?

The connector uses Etsy’s official API with the sign-in approval Etsy provides for connected apps. You grant access yourself, and you can revoke it in your Etsy settings at any time.

Can it change my listings or prices?

No. The Etsy Connector is read-only: snapshots, orders, quantities, reviews, and your ledger. Nothing in your shop is edited.

What counts as the “shop snapshot”?

A summary built from your receipts for the period you ask about — sales total, order count, and top items — compared with the previous period when you ask for a comparison.

What happens at the end of the free month?

The connector pauses until you subscribe. Your connection stays in place, so subscribing resumes everything instantly.
