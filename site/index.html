Why Plugvana

Built for owners who would rather ask a question than open a dashboard.

Free month, honestly

Every connector is free for its first month. No card to start, and when the month ends it pauses — it never charges silently.

No bundles, ever

Pay only for the connectors you use. Each one is priced and subscribed to on its own — no all-access pass, no forced bundle.

Your data stays yours

Connectors run inside your own Muse. This store never receives your orders, customers, or revenue figures — only install and subscription status.

Approval before action

Read-only by default. Anything that would change something is shown to you first and runs only after you approve it in your own Muse.
