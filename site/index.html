Why connectors?

Your data stays yours

A connector runs inside your own Muse. This store never sees your store data, your customers, or your campaigns — there is nothing for us to store.

Read-only by default

Connectors start by only looking. Anything that changes something — a coupon, a price — is shown to you first and needs your approval in your own Muse.

Credentials never pass through chat

You sign in on the service’s own secure page, or paste a key you created into Muse’s secure connection flow. Never into a chat message.

Real software, not a prompt pack

Each connector is a maintained piece of software with versioned releases — the same class of product as the connectors Muse ships for other services.

Simple pricing

  • Every connector is free for the first month. No card needed to start the trial.
  • After the trial, each connector has its own monthly price — WooCommerce $19/month, Etsy $9/month, Mailchimp $15/month — you only pay for the ones you use.
  • No bundles and no all-access pass — every connector stands on its own, so you never pay for ones you don't use.
  • Billing runs through Stripe. Cancel anytime — cancelling just stops the connector at the end of the paid period.

Common questions

Is this store official?

No. This is an independent store run by CALL SNAP SYSTEMS LLC. Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta, WooCommerce, Automattic, Etsy, or Mailchimp.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere new. The connector runs inside your own Muse and talks to the service you connected. The store never receives your store data.

What happens when the free month ends?

The connector pauses until you subscribe. Nothing is charged automatically — there is no card on file unless you chose to add one.
