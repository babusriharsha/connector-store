How to connect WooCommerce to Muse

Muse doesn’t connect to WooCommerce on its own — you add a connector. The ShopSignal for WooCommerce from Plugvana links your store to your Muse personal AI agent, and your own Muse does the installing. Here is the whole process:

Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta or with any platform our connectors work with.

1

Open your Muse and say it

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my WooCommerce store with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds the ShopSignal for WooCommerce in this store and presents it to you as a card — tap Connect on the card to start.

2

Create REST API keys in WP Admin

Your Muse will ask you to create keys under WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API. Enter them through Muse’s secure connection flow — never paste keys into a chat message.

3

Confirm with a live snapshot

Your Muse runs a health check and shows you today’s revenue, orders, and top product from your real store. If they match your dashboard, you’re connected — start asking questions.

After that, try: “What sold today compared with yesterday?”, “Which products are running low?”, or “Give me my Morning Store Brief.” The connector is free for the first month, no card needed.
