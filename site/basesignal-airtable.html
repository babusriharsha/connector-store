How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Airtable with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Airtable with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • An Airtable account and a personal access token, saved through Muse's secure flow.
  • The base you want to start with (you can add more later).

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $15/month per base (or $150/year — two months free). When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • Token via secure flow only. Never paste it into chat.
  • Read-first. Record creation is the only write and it is approval-gated.
  • Your base data stays between Airtable and your Muse.

BaseSignal for Airtable — questions

Which base does the subscription cover?

One base per subscription — the one you connect first. Add another base with its own subscription.

Can it delete records?

No delete commands exist in this connector.

Do formulas and attachments show?

Computed fields come back as their values; attachments are listed by name and URL.
