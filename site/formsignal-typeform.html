How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Typeform with Plugvana”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Typeform with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then complete the secure connection flow. Your Muse confirms the connection with a live check of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent.
  • A Typeform account you administer.
  • Your Typeform personal access token, saved through Muse's secure flow.

Safety notes

  • Token via secure flow only.
  • Read-only. This connector cannot change or delete responses — by design.
  • Respondent data stays between Typeform and your Muse. Treat answers as personal data; Muse summarises, never shares onward.

FormSignal for Typeform — questions

Can it create forms?

No — FormSignal is deliberately read-only in v1.

Does it show every answer field?

Latest responses are summarised with the key fields; ask for a specific response to see it in full.

Hidden fields and UTMs?

They're part of response data and can be included on request.
