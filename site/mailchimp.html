Mailchimp Connector

Connect your Mailchimp account to your Muse personal AI agent. Ask “Is my list growing?”, “How did my last campaign actually do?”, “Which campaign performed best this quarter?” — and get plain numbers, not a dashboard tour. Looking for steps? See how to connect Mailchimp to Muse.

What it does

Audience growth

Subscriber counts per audience, growth or shrinkage over any period, and where the change came from — signups vs unsubscribes.

Campaign reports in plain numbers

Opens, clicks, and bounces for any campaign, stated plainly and compared with your own averages — so “good” actually means something.

Top campaigns

Your best campaigns by opens, clicks, or engagement over any period — the ones worth learning from and repeating.

List health checks

Bounce and unsubscribe patterns, stale segments, and audiences that need attention — flagged before they hurt your deliverability.

Draft campaigns — never sent

Your Muse can prepare a draft campaign in Mailchimp for you to review. Drafts are never sent, scheduled, or published without you doing it yourself.

Period summaries

“This month vs last month” across sends, opens, clicks, and list growth — your whole email program in one short read.

How to connect

Open your Muse and say: “Connect my Mailchimp account with The Connector Store”

1

Open your Muse

Start a chat with the Muse personal AI agent you already use — there is nothing to download from this site.

2

Say the sentence

Say “Connect my Mailchimp account with The Connector Store”. Your Muse finds this connector, and presents it to you as a card with its icon, name and a Connect button.

3

Tap Connect & sign in

Tap Connect on the card your Muse shows you, then sign in on Mailchimp’s own secure page (or through Muse’s secure connection flow). Your Muse confirms the connection with a live snapshot of your real data.

Requirements

  • A Muse personal AI agent (the one you already chat with).
  • A Mailchimp account where you can create API keys (account admin access).
  • A couple of minutes to create the key when your Muse asks.
  • No card needed for the free month.

Pricing

Free for 1 month from activation — full features, no card required to start.

Then a per-connector monthly price. $15/month per account and shown here before subscriptions open. When the trial ends without a subscription, the connector pauses — it never charges silently. See Pricing.

Safety notes

  • Credentials never pass through chat. Your API key goes through Muse’s secure connection flow only.
  • Reports are read-only. Reading audiences and campaign reports changes nothing in your account.
  • Drafts only — never sends. The connector can prepare a draft campaign for your review. Sending, scheduling, and publishing always stay in your hands, inside Mailchimp.
  • Your data stays yours. The connector runs inside your own Muse. This store never receives your subscriber lists or campaign data — only install and trial/subscription status.
  • Revoke anytime. Delete the API key in Mailchimp and ask your Muse to remove the connector; access ends immediately.

Mailchimp Connector — questions

Can this connector send a campaign by accident?

No. It is built to create drafts only. There is no send, schedule, or publish action in the connector — those buttons stay in Mailchimp, under your hand.

What does “plain numbers” mean?

Instead of a dashboard, you get sentences: “Your last campaign reached 4,120 subscribers: 38% opened, 3.1% clicked, 0.4% bounced — opens above your 31% average.” Compared with your own history, so the numbers mean something.

Does it work with multiple audiences?

Yes. Ask about one audience or all of them; your Muse will name which audience each number belongs to.

What happens at the end of the free month?

The connector pauses until you subscribe. Your key and setup stay in place, so subscribing resumes everything instantly.
