Privacy Policy

Plugvana is a product of CALL SNAP SYSTEMS LLC, Eureka, Missouri, USA. Questions about this policy or your data: vbshmedia@gmail.com.

Last updated: November 24, 2026

How our connectors work — and why we see so little

Our connectors run inside your own Muse, on your machine. When a connector reads your store, shop, or account, that data moves between the platform and your Muse. It does not pass through our servers. Your platform credentials (API keys, sign-ins) are entered only through Muse’s secure connection flow and are never sent to us, never shown in chat, and never written to our systems.

What we collect

To run trials and subscriptions, our license service collects only licensing metadata:

  • Install ID — a random identifier generated on your machine when a connector is installed.
  • Shop hash — a one-way cryptographic hash of your store URL, shop ID, or account ID. It lets us tie a trial or license to one shop without storing the shop’s identity itself; it cannot be reversed to recover your store’s address or ID.
  • Product and version of the connector, its activation and trial dates, and check-in timestamps (the connector confirms its license status at most once a day).
  • License key hash — we store a hash of your license key, not the key itself.
  • Subscription status mirrored from Stripe (for example: trialing, active, cancelled), so the connector knows whether to run.
  • Your email address, captured from Stripe Checkout, used to deliver your license key and for billing support. The key-delivery record is deleted once your key has been delivered.

An install ID and a shop hash are pseudonymous identifiers — we treat them as personal data under this policy, and we do not claim we “collect nothing”.

Payments

All payments are processed by Stripe. We never see, receive, or store your card number or other payment-card details. Stripe’s own privacy policy governs the payment information it holds.

What we do NOT collect

  • Your store’s, shop’s, or account’s business data — orders, sales, revenue, listings, campaigns, or customer lists. That data stays in your own Muse.
  • Your platform credentials or API keys.
  • The contents of your conversations with your Muse.

Data processed inside your Muse

When you use a connector, business data — including, for example, your buyers’ names and addresses on Etsy receipts, or your Mailchimp contacts’ details — is fetched and processed by the connector inside your Muse, in your environment, on your instructions. We act only as the provider of the software you run; we do not receive that data, and we process it only as your instructions through the connector direct. You remain responsible for that data and for complying with the privacy laws that apply to your business.

This website

This website is a static site hosted on Vercel. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to operate and secure the site. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Your choices and deletion

You may ask us at any time what licensing data we hold about you, and you may ask us to delete it. Email vbshmedia@gmail.com and we will delete your license and install records, except any records we are required to keep for tax, accounting, or legal purposes. Deleting your records ends the associated trial or subscription.

Security

License keys are stored only as hashes, license check-ins travel over encrypted connections, and access to the license service is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but our design goal is simple: the less we hold, the less can ever be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be announced on this site before they take effect.
