How it works

A connector is a small piece of software that lets your Muse personal AI agent reach a service you already use — and act on what it finds, with your approval. Installing one takes minutes, and your own Muse does the work.

Independent store. Not affiliated with Meta or with any platform our connectors work with.

The three steps

1

Pick your connector

Browse the connector catalog and open the page for the service you want to connect — WooCommerce, Etsy, or Mailchimp. The page lists exactly what the connector can do, what it needs, and what it costs.

2

Say one sentence to your Muse

Open your Muse and say what you want to connect — for example, “Connect my WooCommerce store with Plugvana”. Your Muse finds the right connector in this store, presents it to you as a card, and installs it when you tap Connect.

3

Sign in once — then just ask

You complete sign-in on the service’s own secure page (or with a key you created, entered through Muse’s secure connection flow — never in chat). Your Muse runs a health check and confirms the connection with a live snapshot of your real data. From then on, you simply ask questions.

After you’re connected

Asking is the interface

  • “What sold today, compared with yesterday?”
  • “Which products are about to run out?”
  • “How did my last email campaign do — in plain numbers?”
  • “Give me my Morning Store Brief.”

Safety is built in

  • Connectors are read-only by default — they look, they don’t touch.
  • Any action that changes something (a coupon, a price, a draft) is shown to you first and runs only after your approval in your own Muse.
  • Your data stays between you, your Muse, and the service you connected. This store never sees it.
  • Disconnect any time: ask your Muse to remove the connector, and revoke access on the service’s side too.

The free month

Every connector is free for its first month, starting the day you activate it. No card is needed to start. When the month ends, the connector pauses — it never charges you silently. Subscribe only if it earned its place. See pricing for the full picture.
